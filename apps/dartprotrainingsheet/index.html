Features

Train with a plan. See what it does for your game.

One sheet, played in order

Eight training games follow one after another. Finish one and the next opens up, while every result adds to your total.

Tap in your hits

Throw your visit, then enter the hits with one tap. Big targets and big buttons you can use straight from the oche.

See where your darts land

A hit map on the board, your accuracy for every target and your best visit, so you know which numbers need work.

One final score

Every game adds its points to the sheet. Finish all eight and you get one final score that shows how you threw today, compared with your usual level and with everyone else on the leaderboard.

Leaderboard

Put your score next to everyone else's.

Finished sheets go straight to the public leaderboard. Check your rank for all time or a single period.
