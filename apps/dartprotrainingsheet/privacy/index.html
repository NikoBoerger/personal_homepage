Information Collection and Use

I do not run a server of my own, and the App has no user accounts of its own. Your training data stays on your device unless you decide to move it or back it up.

The App uses third party services for advertising, analytics, crash reports and, on Android, for the leaderboard, sign-in and backup. These services may collect information that can identify you. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy:

Data Stored on Your Device

Your training sheets, games, visits, scores, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to me.

If you use your device’s own backup (iCloud or computer backups on iOS, Android Auto Backup to your Google account), this data can be part of that backup. These backups are handled by Apple or Google under their terms, and I have no access to them.

Google Sign-In and Drive Backup (Android)

On Android, you can optionally sign in with your Google account to back up your training sheets to Google Drive or to sync them between devices. Signing in is only needed for these features. Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication, which processes your Google account identifier, email address and name. The App shows your name and email address on the Transfer screen and stores an account identifier on your device so that sync stays connected to the same account.

The App can only access files it created itself in your Google Drive. A backup is saved in your Drive in the folder “Dart Pro Training Sheet (Backups)” and contains your finished sheets (dates, scores, games and visits), your device model, the platform and the app version. The App keeps the five newest backups and deletes older ones. With auto-sync (Premium, off by default), the App keeps a sync file in that folder up to date, including a list of deleted sheets so that deletions reach your other devices.

These files are stored in your own Google Drive, not on a server operated by me, and I cannot read them. You can sign out on the Transfer screen at any time and revoke the App’s access in your Google account settings.

The iOS version has no sign-in and no Google Drive backup.

Leaderboard (Android)

The leaderboard runs on Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Play Games, the App submits the final score of every finished sheet that passes a plausibility check. The App sends only the score. On the leaderboard, your score appears with your Play Games profile name and avatar, according to the visibility settings of your Play Games profile, which also decide whether friends can compare scores with you.

You can manage your Play Games profile, its visibility and its data in the Play Games settings of your device or Google account. The iOS version has no leaderboard and submits no scores.

Transfer and Sharing

File export and import. You can export your sheets as a file and send it through your device’s share sheet, or import such a file. The file contains your sheets (dates, scores, games and visits) and no personal identifiers.

QR code transfer. Your sheets are encoded into a QR code shown on one device and scanned with the camera of the other device. No server is involved. The App uses the camera only while you scan and does not store camera images.

Result image. The share image is created on your device. You decide whether to send it through the share sheet or save it to your photo library, for which iOS asks for your permission. When you take a screenshot, the App may offer to share the result image instead. It does not read or upload the screenshot.

Advertising

The App is funded by ads delivered through Google AdMob. To show ads and measure their performance, AdMob and its advertising partners may process your device’s advertising identifier, advertising data (such as which ads you have seen or tapped), your approximate location derived from your IP address, and similar device and usage information. Premium users are not shown ads.

When you allow tracking, your advertising identifier may be used to link your activity across other companies’ apps and websites in order to show personalized ads. If you do not allow tracking, the App still shows ads, but they are non-personalized and your advertising identifier is not used to track you.

On iOS, the advertising identifier is the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), and the App asks for permission through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

On Android, the advertising identifier is the Google Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it at any time in your device settings under Google, then Ads.

Analytics and Crash Reports

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is used. Analytics processes usage and interaction data, an app instance identifier, device information such as model and operating system version, and your approximate location. The App also logs a few events of its own, for example the final score when you finish a sheet. The App does not send your name, email address or account identifier to Analytics.

Firebase Crashlytics detects and diagnoses crashes and errors. A crash report contains technical information such as the device model, operating system version, the state of the App at the time of the error, the time and date of the error, and an installation identifier.

In-App Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade unlocks all statistics, removes ads and adds features such as auto-sync and QR code scanning. It is sold as a one-time purchase or as a subscription, depending on the platform. Payments are processed by Google Play on Android and by the App Store on iOS, under their own privacy policies. I do not receive your payment details. The App checks your purchase on your device to unlock Premium.

Legal Bases for Processing

I process personal data on the following legal bases under Article 6(1) GDPR:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): personalized advertising and analytics, where consent is required (see “Your Privacy Choices”). You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): the Google Drive backup and sync when you sign in, submitting your score to the leaderboard when you are signed in to Play Games, and processing your Premium purchase.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): crash reports and app stability, protecting the leaderboard against implausible scores, and showing non-personalized ads to fund the free App.

Your Privacy Choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads and analytics are used. You can review or withdraw your consent at any time under “Ad Settings” in the App.

Data Retention

  • Data on your device remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Google Drive backups and sync files remain in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Your Firebase Authentication sign-in record remains until you ask me to delete it.
  • Leaderboard scores are kept by Google Play Games until you delete your Play Games data.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days.
  • Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.
  • Advertising data is kept according to Google’s retention policies.

Deleting Your Data

  • Sheets on your device are removed when you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Google Drive backups (Android) are stored in your own Drive. Delete them there, and revoke the App’s access in your Google account settings.
  • Your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication (Android) is deleted on request. See Delete Account for how to ask.
  • Leaderboard scores (Android) are deleted together with your Play Games data in the Play Games settings.

The App is a native app and does not use browser cookies. The third party services described above use device identifiers and similar technologies, as described in their privacy policies.

I value your trust and use commercially acceptable means to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

The App contains links to other sites, such as the app stores. These sites are not operated by me, and I have no control over their content or privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. It is not part of Google Play’s Families program or Apple’s Kids category, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. In Germany the age of digital consent under the GDPR is 16; in other countries it can be as low as 13. If you are below the age that applies to you, please use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact me so that I can take the necessary action.
