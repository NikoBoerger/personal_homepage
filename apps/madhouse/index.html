Madhouse

Madhouse is a darts scoring app, built by me, a darts player, for darts players. Launched in September 2025, it combines a beautiful, intuitive design with powerful features, making it easy to use without sacrificing any functionality. Whether you’re tracking your scores, analyzing your performance, or competing with friends, Madhouse makes scoring darts simple, elegant, and fun.

Upcoming Features

  • Dart Bot: Play X01 against a bot
  • Improved 2-Player View: Show two players next to each other, if a two-player match is being played
  • Individual Player Settings: Set individual keyboard shortcut buttons and other settings for each player
  • Improved Match Summary: Make it possible to analyze every detail of your match
  • Deletion of Matches: Delete matches that you don't want to include in the statistics
  • Statistic Filters: Extensive filtering options to get the most out of your statistics
  • Cricket: Add Cricket as a second game
