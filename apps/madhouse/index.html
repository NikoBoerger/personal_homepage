Features

Every feature you need. Simple to use.

X01

Big, clear scores you can read from the oche. 501, 301, 170 and more, with sets and legs, double or single out. Two players sit side by side, and any number of friends can join. Every player keeps their own settings, like the keyboard they prefer to score with.

Bots that throw like real players

Choose the average your bot should play. It throws with exactly the accuracy of a player at that level, and just like a real opponent it has its good legs and its bad ones. You never know what's coming.

Cricket, like on a chalkboard

The whole screen is the board: marks drawn in chalk, numbers big enough to read from the oche, points counted for you.

Extensive statistics

See how your game develops: average, first 9 average and a summary for every match. Filter by player, game and time range.

The voice of the stage

Mastercaller Kirk Bevins calls every score.

Every visit, every game shot. Prefer silence? Switch the caller off in the settings.
