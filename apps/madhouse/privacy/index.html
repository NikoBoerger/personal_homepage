Data Stored on Your Device

Your players, matches, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. Madhouse has no user accounts and no server of its own, so this data is never uploaded to us.

If you add a picture to a player, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library. The picture you take or choose is stored only on your device. You can remove it at any time, and all app data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information We Collect

We use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how our app is used and to improve its performance and features. Firebase Analytics automatically collects certain information, which may include:

  • Device information (e.g., model, operating system version, language)
  • App usage data (e.g., screens viewed, sessions, app version)
  • General location information (e.g., country, region), derived from your IP address
  • Online identifiers such as a randomly generated app instance ID and, where available, your device's advertising identifier

We also use Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to find and fix crashes. If the app crashes or runs into a serious error, a crash report is sent. It contains technical information such as the error details, device model, operating system and app version, the state of the device at the time of the crash, and a randomly generated installation ID.

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Madhouse shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How We Use the Information

The information collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics is used solely for:

  • Monitoring app performance and stability
  • Finding and fixing crashes and errors
  • Improving the user experience
  • Understanding how users interact with the app

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Legal Basis

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing a stable, error free app and in improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

International Data Transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area. These transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified, and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Firebase according to Google's retention settings. Crashlytics keeps crash reports and the associated identifiers for 90 days. We do not store this data ourselves.

Your Choices

You can object to the processing at any time by contacting us. On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or delete it in your device settings under Google, then Ads. On iOS, you can manage tracking under Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Uninstalling the app stops all data collection.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • have it corrected (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • have it deleted (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to its processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Unabhängiges Landeszentrum für Datenschutz Schleswig-Holstein (ULD), but you may also contact the authority in your country of residence.

Children's Privacy

Madhouse is a general audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
