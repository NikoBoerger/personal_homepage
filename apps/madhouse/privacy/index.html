Privacy Policy

Effective date: 06.09.2025

Madhouse respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how our app is used and to improve its performance and features. Firebase Analytics automatically collects certain information, which may include:

  • Device information (e.g., model, operating system version)
  • App usage data (e.g., screens viewed, actions taken)
  • General location information (e.g., country, region)

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or precise location.

How We Use the Information

The information collected through Firebase Analytics is used solely for:

  • Monitoring app performance
  • Improving the user experience
  • Understanding how users interact with the app

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses Firebase Analytics, which is provided by Google. For details about how Google handles data in Firebase, please refer to Google's privacy documentation:

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Firebase according to Google’s policies. We do not store this analytics data ourselves.
